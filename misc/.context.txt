* Action indicators

** &&&
&&& indicates a specific incompleteness at that location.
This symbol can be very focused, as in 'function(arg1, &&&)' where the second argument name is not yet established,
or it can indicate a broader incompleteness as in '&&&paragraph needed' where a small task is indicated
The context will determine the scope. A terse note may provide a hint. it serves as a todo and placeholder.

** TODO
TODO indicates broader tasks to accomplish, typically listed in priority as a stack, top is now.
